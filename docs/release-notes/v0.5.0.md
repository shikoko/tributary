A branch's lane lights up when you point at it, actions run in two repositories at once, and everything is easier to read.

## New

- **A branch's lane lights up:** point at a branch in the sidebar, or at a branch or tag label in the graph, and its lane lights up while the rest of the graph fades. Its copies on remotes count too, so a branch that's ahead on origin lights up all the way to its newest commit. Its mark beside the scrollbar glows, so you can find it when it's off screen.
- **The marks beside the scrollbar explain themselves:** point at one for a card saying what it is (your branch, a search match, the selected commit), and the marks under the pointer glow.
- **You can see where you jumped to:** a commit you jump to from the sidebar, a search or a link is outlined for a moment, so your eye finds it.

## Fixes

- **Discard all deletes new files too,** and **Undo** puts them back, for 30 days.
- **Actions run in two repositories at once:** a long pull in one tab no longer stops you fetching in another. Each tab shows its own progress, and **Cancel** stops only its own.
- **Easier to read, in both themes:** faint text, counts and labels are clearer. The checked-out branch's label in the light theme has dark text. Blue and red buttons are a deeper colour, so their white text reads, and the words in buttons sit in the middle.
- **Keyboard focus** is the same clear ring everywhere.
- **Screen readers** can follow the tabs, the sidebar, the commit list, changes and who changed each line.
- **Spacing** is even across the app.
- **The update window stays in front** while an update installs.

## Installing

- **macOS:** the `.dmg`, one app for Apple Silicon and Intel. Open it and drag Tributary to Applications.
- **Windows:** the `-setup.exe` (64-bit): agree to the terms and click Install, or choose **Custom install** to install it for everyone on the PC or in another folder.

Tributary needs [git](https://git-scm.com), and [Git LFS](https://git-lfs.com) for repositories that use it. After this, it updates itself.

The installers aren't code-signed yet, so the first launch needs one extra step:

- **macOS, "Tributary Not Opened":** click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to **Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Tributary. Open it again and choose **Open Anyway**. On macOS 14 and earlier, you can instead Control-click the app in Applications and choose **Open**.
- **macOS, "is damaged" (or it still won't open):** move it to Applications and run `xattr -cr /Applications/Tributary.app` once in Terminal.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns you, choose **More info → Run anyway**.

It's only needed once: the updates Tributary installs itself open straight away.

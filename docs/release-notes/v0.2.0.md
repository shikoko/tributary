The first public release of Tributary: a fast, friendly git client for macOS and Windows, with a commit graph that's easy to read, Undo for almost everything, pull requests in the app, and first-class Git LFS, locks and art diffs.

## Highlights

- **A commit graph that's a pleasure to read:** branches, tags and stashes labelled, search by message, author, file or code, and drag a branch onto another to merge or rebase.
- **Undo for almost everything:** commits, checkouts, merges and deleted branches, with a list of your actions, and branches backed up before they're rewritten or deleted.
- **Local history:** your uncommitted work is kept every few minutes, so a crash or an overwrite doesn't cost you an afternoon.
- **Simple mode:** for artists and designers. Get latest, your changes with their locks, and one Submit button, without branches or the graph.
- **See what changed in your art:** PSD, TGA, DDS, EXR and more, Blender and Unreal thumbnails, 3D models, sounds and video, with swipe, onion skin and changed pixels.
- **Git LFS and locks, handled:** fast downloads with a count of files and the time left, a disk space check first, and locks as part of the everyday flow.
- **Shelves:** share unfinished work with the team without committing it to a branch.
- **Team activity:** who's working on what, the files changed most, and the branches in progress.
- **Pull requests in the app:** reviews, checks, comments and merging, on GitHub and GitLab, with CI status on every commit.
- **Conflicts, resolved in the app:** mine, theirs, both or your own edit for text, and a whole version for assets.
- **Project setup check:** finds what's wrong with an Unreal or Unity project's git setup, and fixes it.
- **Guided tours:** short tours that point at the real thing, offered once, and easy to turn off.

The [README](https://github.com/shikoko/tributary#readme) and the [feature guide](https://github.com/shikoko/tributary/blob/main/docs/features.md) have everything else.

## Installing

- **macOS:** the `.dmg`, one app for Apple Silicon and Intel.
- **Windows:** the `-setup.exe` or the `.msi` (64-bit).

Tributary needs [git](https://git-scm.com), and [Git LFS](https://git-lfs.com) for repositories that use it. After this, it updates itself.

The installers aren't code-signed yet, so the first launch needs one extra step:

- **macOS, "Tributary Not Opened":** click **Done**, then open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to **Security** and click **Open Anyway** next to Tributary. Open it again and choose **Open Anyway**. On macOS 14 and earlier, you can instead Control-click the app in Applications and choose **Open**.
- **macOS, "is damaged" (or it still won't open):** move it to Applications and run `xattr -cr /Applications/Tributary.app` once in Terminal.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen warns you, choose **More info → Run anyway**.

It's only needed once: the updates Tributary installs itself open straight away.
